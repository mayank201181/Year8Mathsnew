YEAR 8 MATHS CURRICULUM COVERAGE MAP
Learner: 12–13 year old at an international school in Singapore that uses UK "Year 8" naming.
Frameworks covered: Cambridge Lower Secondary Maths 0862, Stage 8 (2020 framework, first taught Sept 2021); England KS3 / White Rose Maths Year 8; Singapore MOE Sec 1 as a sanity check.

Tags used below:
- [C8] = Cambridge Stage 8 objective
- [C9] = Cambridge Stage 9 objective (look-ahead)
- [WR8] = White Rose Year 8 small step; (H) = WR "higher" step
- [NC] = KS3 National Curriculum statement
- [SG1] = Singapore Sec 1

1. CAMBRIDGE STAGE 8 OBJECTIVES BY STRAND (paraphrased, complete)
==================================================================

NUMBER: Integers, powers and roots
- 8Ni.01 Order of operations including brackets, indices, and square and cube roots.
- 8Ni.02 Estimate, multiply and divide integers (including negatives), recognising generalisations.
- 8Ni.03 Factors, multiples, prime factors, HCF and LCM.
- 8Ni.04 Hierarchy of number sets: natural numbers ⊂ integers ⊂ rational numbers.
- 8Ni.05 Positive and zero indices; index laws for multiplication and division.
- 8Ni.06 Squares of negative and positive numbers, and the corresponding square roots (± roots).
- 8Ni.07 Positive and negative cube numbers, and their cube roots.

NUMBER: Place value, ordering and rounding
- 8Np.01 Multiply and divide integers and decimals by 0.1 and 0.01.
- 8Np.02 Round to a given number of significant figures.

NUMBER: Fractions, decimals, percentages, ratio and proportion
- 8Nf.01 Recognise fractions that are equivalent to recurring decimals.
- 8Nf.02 Estimate and subtract mixed numbers; give the answer as a mixed number in simplest form.
- 8Nf.03 Estimate and multiply an integer by a mixed number; divide an integer by a proper fraction.
- 8Nf.04 Use the laws of arithmetic and order of operations to simplify decimal and fraction calculations.
- 8Nf.05 Percentage increase and decrease, and absolute change.
- 8Nf.06 Compare and order positive and negative decimals and fractions using =, ≠, >, <, ≤, ≥.
- 8Nf.07 Estimate and multiply decimals by integers and by decimals.
- 8Nf.08 Estimate and divide decimals by numbers with one decimal place.
- 8Nf.09 Relationship between ratio and direct proportion.
- 8Nf.10 Simplify and compare ratios, including quantities in different units.
- 8Nf.11 Use ratios to compare quantities; divide an amount in a ratio with 2 or more parts.

ALGEBRA: Expressions, equations and formulae
- 8Ae.01 Letters mean different things in expressions, formulae and equations.
- 8Ae.02 Laws of arithmetic and order of operations apply to algebraic terms (four operations, squares, cubes).
- 8Ae.03 Expand a single term over a bracket (including squares and cubes); factorise by taking out the HCF.
- 8Ae.04 Words ↔ algebraic expressions (linear, with integer or fractional coefficients).
- 8Ae.05 Words ↔ formulae (mixed operations); change the subject using inverse operations.
- 8Ae.06 Words ↔ equations; solve linear equations with integer or fractional coefficients and the unknown on one or both sides.
- 8Ae.07 Letters can represent open and closed intervals (two terms), e.g. −2 < x ≤ 5.

ALGEBRA: Sequences, functions and graphs
- 8As.01 Term-to-term rules; generate sequences from numerical and spatial patterns (including fractions).
- 8As.02 nth term in the forms n ± a, an, an ± b, where a and b are positive or negative integers or fractions.
- 8As.03 A function gives each input a single output; find outputs and, using inverse operations, inputs (including fractions).
- 8As.04 Words ↔ linear function y = mx + c.
- 8As.05 Tables of values; plot graphs of y = mx + c.
- 8As.06 y = mx + c is a straight line with m = gradient and c = y-intercept (integer m).
- 8As.07 Read and interpret graphs with more than one component; explain their shape and what intersections mean (real-life graphs).

GEOMETRY & MEASURE: Geometrical reasoning, shapes and measurements
- 8Gg.01 Hierarchy of quadrilaterals.
- 8Gg.02 π as circumference ÷ diameter; know and use C = πd. (Area of a circle is Stage 9.)
- 8Gg.03 Miles and kilometres: 1 km ≈ 5/8 mile; 1 mile ≈ 1.6 km.
- 8Gg.04 Derive and use area formulae for parallelograms and trapezia.
- 8Gg.05 Euler's formula V + F − E = 2 (polyhedra only).
- 8Gg.06 Derive and use the volume of a triangular prism.
- 8Gg.07 Draw front, side and top views (plans and elevations) of 3D shapes to scale.
- 8Gg.08 Surface area of cubes, cuboids, triangular prisms and pyramids.
- 8Gg.09 For a regular polygon, number of sides = number of lines of symmetry = order of rotational symmetry.
- 8Gg.10 Exterior angle of a triangle = sum of the two interior opposite angles.
- 8Gg.11 Angles on parallel and intersecting lines: alternate, corresponding, vertically opposite.
- 8Gg.12 Construct triangles, the midpoint and perpendicular bisector of a segment, and an angle bisector.

GEOMETRY & MEASURE: Position and transformation
- 8Gp.01 Bearings as a measure of direction.
- 8Gp.02 Midpoint of a line segment from coordinates.
- 8Gp.03 Translate by a vector; the image is congruent.
- 8Gp.04 Reflect in lines on or parallel to the axes, or in y = ±x; identify a reflection and its mirror line.
- 8Gp.05 Rotations need a centre, a direction and an angle; perform and identify them.
- 8Gp.06 Enlarge by a positive integer scale factor from a centre outside or on the shape; identify the scale factor.

STATISTICS & PROBABILITY: Statistics
- 8Ss.01 Select, trial and justify data collection and sampling methods; categorical, discrete and continuous data.
- 8Ss.02 Advantages and disadvantages of different sampling methods.
- 8Ss.03 Record and represent data, and choose and justify a representation from:
  - Venn and Carroll diagrams
  - tally charts, frequency tables and two-way tables
  - dual and compound bar charts
  - pie charts
  - frequency diagrams for continuous data
  - line graphs and time-series graphs
  - scatter graphs
  - stem-and-leaf diagrams
  - infographics
- 8Ss.04 Compare two distributions using mode, median, mean and range, considering centre and spread together.
- 8Ss.05 Interpret patterns, trends and relationships; discuss conclusions, sources of variation (including sampling), and check predictions.

STATISTICS & PROBABILITY: Probability
- 8Sp.01 Complementary events have probabilities that total 1.
- 8Sp.02 Use tables, diagrams and lists to find all mutually exclusive outcomes of combined independent events (sample spaces).
- 8Sp.03 Theoretical probabilities of equally likely combined events.
- 8Sp.04 Design and run experiments or simulations with small and large numbers of trials; compare experimental and theoretical probability.

THINKING & WORKING MATHEMATICALLY (not a separate strand; built into all topics)
- TWM.01 Specialising: choose an example to test whether it meets given criteria.
- TWM.02 Generalising: spot an underlying pattern across many examples.
- TWM.03 Conjecturing: form mathematical questions or ideas.
- TWM.04 Convincing: present evidence to justify or challenge an idea or solution.
- TWM.05 Characterising: identify and describe the mathematical properties of an object.
- TWM.06 Classifying: group objects by their mathematical properties.
- TWM.07 Critiquing: compare and evaluate ideas, representations or solutions.
- TWM.08 Improving: refine an approach or representation to make it more effective.

2. WHITE ROSE MATHS YEAR 8 BLOCKS (current scheme)
==================================================================

AUTUMN
1. Ratio & scale: ratio notation, 1:n, m:n, dividing in a ratio, simplest form, ratio vs fraction, π as a ratio, gradient as a ratio (H).
2. Multiplicative change: direct proportion, conversion graphs, currency, direct proportion graphs (H), similar shapes, scale factors, scale diagrams, maps.
3. Multiplying & dividing fractions: fraction × integer, fraction × fraction, integer ÷ fraction, reciprocals, mixed numbers, algebraic fractions (H).
4. Working in the Cartesian plane: four quadrants, x = a and y = b, y = x, y = kx and direct proportion, y = x + a, negative gradients, linking to sequences, y = mx + c, non-linear graphs (H), midpoints (H).
5. Representing data: scatter graphs, correlation, line of best fit, non-linear relationships, data types, grouped and ungrouped frequency tables, grouped and continuous data, two-way tables.
6. Tables & probability: sample spaces, two-way tables, Venn diagram probabilities, the product rule for counting outcomes.

SPRING
7. Brackets, equations & inequalities: forming expressions, directed numbers in algebra, expanding and factorising single brackets, expanding binomials (H), equations with brackets, forming and solving inequalities, unknowns on both sides (H), identities vs equations vs formulae.
8. Sequences: rules in words, algebraic rules, nth term of a linear sequence (H).
9. Indices: like terms with indices, multiplication and division laws, power of a power (H).
10. Fractions & percentages: FDP conversions, percentages over 100%, multipliers, percentage change, original amount / reverse percentage (H).
11. Standard index form: positive and negative powers of 10, conversions, ordering, the four operations, calculator use, negative and fractional indices (H).
12. Number sense: rounding to 1 s.f. and to decimal places, estimation, error intervals (H), order of operations, money, metric conversions including area and volume units (H), time and calendar.

SUMMER
13. Angles in parallel lines & polygons: angle rules, alternate/corresponding/co-interior angles, constructing triangles and quadrilaterals, quadrilateral properties and diagonals, exterior and interior angle sums, simple proofs (H), angle bisector (H), perpendicular bisector (H).
14. Area of trapezia & circles: triangles, parallelograms, trapezia, compound shapes, circle area and parts of circles, with and without a calculator.
15. Line symmetry & reflection: line symmetry; reflection in horizontal, vertical and diagonal lines.
16. The data handling cycle: enquiry, questionnaires and bias, bar/multiple bar/pie/line charts, choosing a diagram, grouped data, range, comparing distributions, misleading graphs.
17. Measures of location: mean, median and mode, choosing an average, mean from frequency tables (H), outliers, comparing distributions.

Relevant KS3 NC statements for Year 8:
- standard form A × 10ⁿ
- error intervals a < x ≤ b
- compound units (speed, unit pricing, density)
- direct and inverse proportion including graphs
- scale diagrams and maps
- ruler-and-compass constructions
- area of circles and composite shapes
- polygon angle sums
- Pythagoras (KS3, usually taught in Year 9)
- Venn diagrams and sample spaces
- y = mx + c, gradient and intercept
- scatter graphs

Notable differences between the frameworks:
- In WR/KS3 Year 8 but Cambridge Stage 9: circle area, standard form, solving inequalities and polygon angle sums.
- In Cambridge Stage 8 but not WR Year 8: Euler's formula, plans and elevations, surface area of pyramids, miles/km, bearings, rotations and enlargements.

3. GAP ANALYSIS AGAINST THE 14 EXISTING TOPICS
==================================================================
I ran a keyword scan over lib/topics/*.ts and lib/extras/*.ts.

ABSENT (no matches anywhere):
- Standard form / powers of 10 (WR8 block 11; C9 look-ahead).
- Plans and elevations, front/side/top views (8Gg.07).
- Euler's formula (8Gg.05).
- Hierarchy of number sets (8Ni.04) and hierarchy of quadrilaterals (8Gg.01): the keyword "hierarchy" does not appear.
- Carroll diagrams, dual/compound bar charts, time-series graphs, infographics, frequency diagrams for continuous data (all named in 8Ss.03).
- Frequency polygons (C9; stretch).
- Order of rotational symmetry and the regular-polygon symmetry rule (8Gg.09).
- Complementary events, keyword "complementary" (8Sp.01). The idea may be taught without the word, but it needs to be explicit.
- Absolute change vs percentage change (8Nf.05).
- Error intervals / bounds (WR8 H; C9).
- Simultaneous equations (C9; stretch only).

UNDER-COVERED or in the wrong place:
- Circles: only inside perimeter-area-volume. Needs π as a ratio, circumference, area, semicircles/quarter circles and compound shapes (a full WR block).
- Constructions and bearings: bundled into angles-polygons. Needs its own home with triangle constructions (SSS/SAS/ASA), the bisectors, three-figure and back bearings, and scale drawings/maps (8Gg.12, 8Gp.01).
- Scale drawings and map scales: only in ratio-proportion.
- Compound measures and units: speed, density and unit pricing are inside ratio-proportion. There is no coherent rates/units topic covering km/h ↔ m/s, metric area/volume unit conversion, miles ↔ km (8Gg.03) or time.
- Straight-line and real-life graphs: crammed into sequences-graphs with sequences and functions. Needs its own topic for:
  - four quadrants, x = a, y = b, y = ±x
  - tables of values, y = mx + c, gradient/intercept
  - midpoint (8Gp.02)
  - direct proportion graphs y = kx
  - conversion and distance-time graphs
  - multi-component graphs and intersections (8As.07)
- Function notation and function machines with inverse operations including fractions (8As.03): currently minimal.
- Inequalities: only lightly inside equations. Needs interval notation, number lines with open/closed circles and two-sided intervals (8Ae.07), plus forming and solving (WR8).
- Changing the subject of a formula (8Ae.05): the phrase "subject of" does not appear; only "rearrange" mentions.
- Venn diagrams: only in factors-multiples (HCF/LCM). Missing for data (8Ss.03) and probability (WR8 block 6).
- Sampling methods and their pros and cons, bias, questionnaires (8Ss.01/02): thin.
- Comparing two distributions using centre and spread together (8Ss.04): the averages content needs this explicit comparison skill.
- Product rule for counting outcomes (WR8): not evident.
- Surface area of pyramids and triangular prisms (8Gg.08), and volume of a triangular prism derived (8Gg.06): verify depth in perimeter-area-volume.
- Transformations: verify that centre of rotation, the y = ±x mirror lines, centre outside/on the shape for enlargement, and describing a single transformation fully are all present.
- Pythagoras sits in a Year 8 topic name but is C9 / WR Year 9. Keep it, but as stretch only.
- Multiplying and dividing by 0.1 and 0.01 (8Np.01) and dividing by one-decimal-place numbers (8Nf.08): verify explicit drills.
- Recurring decimals: present; fine.

- Number: primes and prime factorisation; HCF/LCM; squares, cubes and roots by prime factorisation; negative and rational numbers; <, >, ≤, ≥; rounding to d.p./s.f. and estimation. All covered by topics 1, 2 and 5.
